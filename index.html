SMS Backup Converter

Moves your texts, picture messages and group chats to another phone, even when the two phones save their backups in different formats: Fig, TAK, Wonder, Pom, Buggy Backup and SMS Backup & Restore (any Android phone, including the MindPhone).

🔒 Works offline. Your messages never leave this device.

1 Open your backup

Choose the backup from your old phone: the messages-YYYY-MM-DD.zip of a Fig or TAK (SMS Import / Export), an SMS Backup & Restore sms-….xml file, or a Wonder, Pom or Buggy Backup ZIP file. An unzipped Fig or TAK backup works too: choose the folder that contains messages.ndjson.

    4 Create the backup files

    Make the small test file first and restore it on the new phone. If it looks right, make and restore the full backup; messages that are already on the phone are skipped.

    5 Restore on your new phone

      Good to know
      • The MindPhone restores with SMS Backup & Restore: choose that format.
      • A phone that already has messages keeps them. Restoring adds the old messages, files them into the same chats, and skips any message that is already there, so nothing shows up twice.
      • Moving between a Fig and a TAK? They save messages the same way: copy the backup to the other phone as it is. Convert only to leave things out.
      • Several parts: restore part 1 first, then the others in order. Each one adds its messages; nothing is lost.
      • Keep your original backup until you have checked the restored messages.
      If the messages don't show up

      Wait a minute and restart the phone. If they are still missing, your phone's messaging app may want a real SIM id instead of "unknown": send one text on the new phone, make a messages backup with SMS Backup & Restore, open that file in a text editor and note the number in sub_id="…". Then come back here, choose More options → Use this id with that number, and create the files again.
